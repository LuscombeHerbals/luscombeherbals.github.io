About Luscombe Herbals

Luscombe Herbals is a small family operation rooted in Central Queensland. We prepare native and traditional herbs with care, minimal intervention, and a deep respect for the plants and the people who use them.

James & Tash

We're James and Tash, the team behind Luscombe Herbals. Based in Clermont, Queensland, we've been involved in the local community for years and have a deep appreciation for the native plants that grow in our region.

In late 2025, we were entrusted with continuing Steve's Gumby Gumby operation after Steve stepped away. Steve had been preparing Gumby Gumby in Clermont for over 13 years and generously shared his knowledge with us.

Our connection to Steve's Gumby Gumby

We're proud to carry on the work that Steve started. Our commitment is to maintain the same quality, care, and reasonable pricing that Steve's customers have come to expect over more than a decade.

We encourage everyone to do their own research when considering any supplements, and we're always happy to answer questions.

Where we are

Luscombe Herbals is based in Clermont, Queensland, on the traditional lands of the Birri and Gunggari peoples. We acknowledge and pay our respects to Elders past and present.
