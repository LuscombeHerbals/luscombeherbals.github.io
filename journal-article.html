What is Gumby Gumby?

Gumby Gumby (Pittosporum angustifolium) is a small tree or shrub native to arid and semi-arid regions of Australia. It has been used for generations in traditional Aboriginal bush medicine.

The plant is characterised by its long, narrow leaves and small yellow flowers that develop into orange woody capsules. It is a resilient species, well adapted to the harsh Australian climate.

People are drawn to Gumby Gumby for many different reasons, and some choose to include it as part of their personal approach to wellbeing. The leaves are typically harvested, dried, and prepared in various ways – including as a tea, in capsules, or infused into oils.

Traditional use

Gumby Gumby has a long history of use in traditional Aboriginal bush medicine. It is one of many native plants that have been used for generations across various regions of Australia.
