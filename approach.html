Our Approach

We believe in working with plants in a way that respects their nature, their environment, and the people who use them.

Sourcing & harvesting

Our Gumby Gumby is carefully identified and harvested locally near Clermont, Queensland. We take care to select only healthy leaves from thriving trees, ensuring the plant populations remain sustainable.

For our other herbs, we source high-quality ingredients from trusted Australian suppliers, using certified organic ingredients wherever available.

Processing

We process our herbs with minimal intervention. Gumby Gumby leaves are naturally dried and then cool-milled to preserve their integrity. This gentle approach helps maintain the natural characteristics of the plant material.

All capsule products use size 00 capsules. Our balms and soaps are made in small batches using traditional methods.

Small-batch, made with care

Every batch we prepare is made in small quantities. This allows us to maintain quality control, pay attention to every detail, and ensure that what reaches you is the best we can offer.

We aim to keep our products available at a reasonable price, because we believe good herbal remedies should be accessible.
