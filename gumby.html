About Gumby Gumby

Gumby Gumby is a small tree or shrub native to arid and semi-arid regions of Australia. It has been used for generations in traditional Aboriginal bush medicine.

People are drawn to it for many different reasons, and some choose to include it as part of their personal approach to wellbeing.

How we work with Gumby Gumby

Harvesting

We carefully identify and harvest Gumby Gumby leaves locally near Clermont, Queensland.

Drying

Leaves are naturally dried to preserve their integrity and active compounds.

Milling

Dried leaves are cool-milled into a fine powder, then encapsulated with minimal intervention.
